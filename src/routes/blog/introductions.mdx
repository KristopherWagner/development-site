In 2016 after shoveling my car and my girlfriend's car out of 31" of snow, I noticed that the car
parkec outside of my apartment had a handicap tag on the rearview mirror. So I shoveled the snow
around a third car. The next morning, I couldn't get out of bed because my back hurt that much. So,
I found a job in Florida and haven't touched a snow shovel since.

My name is Kristopher Wagner and I hope my home page gave you an overview of who I am; if you
skipped it, I am a family man (husband and father), runner, maker, vegetarian, and maven. I'm the
type of person who reads tech specs, professional reviews, user reviews, and then waits a month
before buying something to make sure I get the most eco-friendly, reliable product. I watch
YouTube videos and read research papers to design the perfect breakfast. And I measure my coffee
to the gram before putting it in a $12 French Press to start my day with the perfect cup of cold
brew each day. I also get paid to write code.

I am a little nervous writing a blog, and I don't know how consistent I'll be (sorry!); but I hope
to share some of my favorite products, walk you through problems I've solved, and maybe teach you
something interesting. Absolute worst case, I hope to write at least one thing that will embarass
my son.

If you read an article about running, know that I have been running since 2005 when I saw Jerome
Bettis sitting on the sideline with an oxygen mask on his face. I asked my dad what was wrong, and
he told me that Bettis had asthma. I had been struggling with asthma for a few years and that day
I decided that if he could play in the NFL with asthma, I could live a normal life with it.

If you read an article about finance or a product review, know that I struggled with money for a
large portion of my life. I graduated with 6-figures of college loans and 5-figures of credit card
debt, so I know what's it like to not be able to afford rent and debt payments (this was another
reason I moved to Florida). Currently our only debt is our mortgage and my wife's car. I promise
that if I recommend a product, it is worth your hard earned time and money.
